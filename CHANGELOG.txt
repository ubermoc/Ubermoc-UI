UbermocUI 1.0.106
- Controller: put the pointer on a number in the settings and press D-pad left / right to change it. Hold to keep going; it speeds up after 1.5 and 3 seconds.
- Controller: holding A on -, +, < or > (and the list Up / Down buttons) now repeats, the same as holding the mouse button. Before, A only ever made one step.
- Every hold-to-repeat now uses one timing: 0.5 seconds before it starts, then every 0.1 seconds (the game's own scroll arrows use the same).
- Number settings with a wide range now stop at their own ends instead of running past them.
- The pointer hint line now says that left / right changes a number.
